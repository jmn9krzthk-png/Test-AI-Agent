    New clients

    We're so excited to meet your dog

    Every new guest goes through four steps so we can make sure Almost Home is a comfortable, safe fit.

    1. Create your account

      Register in our client portal and add your dog's info and vaccine records.

    2. We reach out

      Our team contacts you to learn about your dog and answer questions.

    3. Tour & trial day

      A full day of daycare so we can see how your dog plays, rests and settles.

    4. Book stays

      Once approved, request daycare and boarding right from the portal.

    Comfort

    No signs of extreme discomfort or anxiety after an adjustment period.

    Sociability

    Interacts positively and appropriately with dogs and humans.

    Respect

    Reads social cues and respects boundaries with dogs and staff.

    Self restraint

    Plays without excessive jumping, nipping, mounting, barking or barging.

    Sharing

    Shares water bowls, staff, friends and space without guarding.

    Rest

    Settles in a kennel without constant barking, scratching or whining.

    Deal breakers

    • Aggression toward dogs or people
    • Extreme anxiety or distress

    • Resource guarding
